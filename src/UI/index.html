QVAC 2.1.4-LTS (Quantized)
Daemon Active · Local Execution
The QVAC (Quantized Vision & Analysis Core) runs entirely on your local machine. It continuously monitors your RGB ledger, extracts semantic data from incoming invoices, and audits financial transactions against your historical patterns to prevent fraud and automatically compute compliance margins.
Background Services
Automated Data Extraction
Continuously parses imported PDFs via local vision models to structure amounts, dates, and client identities.
Cryptographic Tamper Detection
Validates original document hashes against ledger commitments to flag semantic tampering (e.g., modified bank details).
RGB Financial Controller
Cross-references incoming transfers to detect anomalies, overpayments, and dynamically computes VAT/Tax liabilities.
Semantic Node Search
Indexes the immutable RGB ledger, allowing you to query complex financial data using natural language.
Security
Payment checks, supporting evidence and operation history

Checks detect specific conditions, not every kind of fraud.
